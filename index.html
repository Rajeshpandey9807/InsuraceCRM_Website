FAQ

Answers for insurance calling leaders

Everything you need to know about onboarding, security, and how InsureCall CRM boosts agent productivity from day one.

Is the CRM really free for unlimited users?

Yes. InsureCall CRM is 100% free for insurance calling teams, with unlimited users, leads, and call history. Paid plans are optional only if you need bespoke integrations or on-prem data residency.

How quickly can my team go live?

Most call floors import their lead sheets and start dialing in under 30 minutes. Need help? Our onboarding desk sets up workflows and role-based access within one business day.

Does it integrate with dialers and policy systems?

We integrate with major auto-dialers, telephony platforms, and insurer policy portals via APIs and webhooks. Custom connectors can be configured for enterprise accounts.

How is data kept secure?

Data is encrypted at rest and in transit, stored on AWS with daily backups, SSO, audit logs, and role-based controls. We comply with IRDAI guidelines and follow rigorous privacy practices.

Can managers track performance in real time?

Yes. Dashboards show connect rates, talk time, conversions, and follow-up queues in real time, helping managers coach agents and fix leakages immediately.
